Creative producer & Narrative filmmaker

Carlota Mac Gregor is a visionary story producer, director, and screenwriter who seamlessly bridges the gap between artistic sensitivity and strategic production. With an MFA in producing from the New York Film Academy and extensive international experience, including roles at Little Studio Films and A Contracorriente Films, and a Bachelor Degree in Directing from Bande A Part Escuela de cine in Barcelona, she brings a global perspective to every project. Carlota is an expert in the full project lifecycle, from development and budgeting to commercial distribution, with a proven track record at prestigious markets and festivals like AFM, San Sebastian, and Sitges. She is dedicated to crafting bold, emotionally resonant narratives that combine complex character-driven storytelling with high-level production management.

The video production process

Free Consultation
↓

I offer a free initial consultation to understand your project's goals, budget, and timeline. This helps us establish the right approach from the start.

Conceptual Development
↓

Working closely with you, I develop the creative concept, script, storyboard and visual style that will best communicate your message.

Pre-Production Planning
↓

Detailed planning including location scouting, casting, equipment prep, scheduling, and logistics to ensure filming day runs smoothly.

Video Production
↓

Professional filming using high-end cameras, lighting and audio equipment. I direct the shoot to capture exactly what is needed.

Post Production Editing
↓

Professional editing, colour grading, sound design, music and graphics to create a polished final product that exceeds expectations.
